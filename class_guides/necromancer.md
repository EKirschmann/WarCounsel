# Necromancer (EQL)

Source: "Multiclass Guide — How It Works & The Best Necromancer Combos
(2026)" by Haitsmelol (necrotalk.com, May 2026) + beta FAQ thread.
Re-verify after patches.

## What Necromancer brings to ANY trio
- Feign Death: reach deep camps, shed aggro, recover bad pulls. EQL
  deaths cost no XP and respawn in-zone, so FD-rushing is low-risk.
- Vampiric Embrace: passive melee lifesteal proc scaling Level + 12
  (72/proc at 60) — stronger for NEC than the SK version of the same
  spell. Free sustain for a melee trio.
- DoTs are among the most mana-efficient damage in the game: best on
  long fights and raid bosses, and they let you hold a defensive stance.
- Pet gear slots PERSIST across summons — keep it geared even in hybrid
  builds.
- Invocations (Recovery, Inversion, Overchannel, Empowering) give a
  melee trio caster invocations it would otherwise have none of.

## Combos that work, and why
- WAR/NEC/BRD — Warrior Defensive Stance (50% melee damage reduction) +
  Necro sustain + Bard Symphonic Aura (free at level 1: auto-pulses up
  to 5 zero-mana non-targeted songs while /melody runs more, ~9
  concurrent). Ogre gets frontal stun immunity free; others pay 50+ AAs.
- WAR/NEC/DRU — durable leveller. Once the Druid is levelled its port
  RITUALS work from any loadout, so it can be swapped out later without
  losing travel.
- MNK/NEC/ROG — deep camps: FD-rush + Rogue Sneak (only top raid bosses
  see through it). Monk Striker Stance (5x special multiplier) + Rogue
  Backstab (works from Primary, Secondary OR Any slot) + DoTs.
- SK+NEC stacks two lifetap procs (Vampiric Embrace + Scream of Death,
  the L39 AoE lifetap proc).
- Multi-pet trios (NEC/MAG/BST) are capped at ONE active pet.

## Notes
- Melee-forward NEC builds beat pure-caster trios for solo sustain.
- Necro lich-style life-for-mana lines do NOT stack with Clarity.
