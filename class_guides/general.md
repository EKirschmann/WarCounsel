# General EQL mechanics & meta

Sources: eqltools.com combat/attributes (client-mined + dev quotes),
necrotalk.com multiclass guide (May 2026), r/EQLegends beta FAQ
(2026-07-14), patch notes 7/7, 7/14, 8/25. Re-verify after patches.

## Multiclass rules
- Trio = primary (race-restricted, LOCKS at 11) + 2 alternates (swap in
  any major city). Levels 1-10 ALL classes gain XP; after 10 only the
  active loadout. EFFECTIVE level = LOWEST of the three — keep synced.
- All spells/abilities/stances/invocations of all three are available
  (one stance + one invocation at a time). Spell Gem Expansion AA is a
  must-buy. Rituals: port/bind/gate of any class you leveled far enough
  work from ANY loadout — level Druid or Wizard to 25 once for ports.
- Primary is usually a tank or healer — the only unswappable slot.

## Pools & stats
- HP/mana/endurance = the TWO highest class pools summed; the third is
  dropped (a third caster adds no mana); formula unpublished.
- Soft caps 200 WIS/INT/CHA (hard 255); ~150 naked ceiling reported;
  pet gear stats cap at 510 total.

## Combat math (client-mined)
- Four numbers: Accuracy+Offense = displayed ATK; Mitigation+Evasion =
  displayed AC. Defenses check riposte -> parry -> dodge -> block, then
  hit roll, then a 20-slot damage roll: Offense pushes the average roll
  up, Mitigation down; endpoints never move. STR over 75 adds ~2/3
  Offense per point (STR/DEX raise the odds of a MAX-damage hit, not
  the damage).
- Skills use the BEST cap in the trio. Mitigation soft-caps per
  class/level; Evasion is uncapped; shield AC BYPASSES that soft cap.
  Haste cap ~175% at 50 (low-level item-haste cap removed 7/7).

## Meta consensus (beta, changes fast)
- A healer is near-mandatory: CLR > SHM/DRU > PAL > RNG/BST. Paladin >
  SK (LoH + undead tools; best non-priest healer).
- A SLOWER (ENC, BRD, SHM, BST) is required for D3+; only exception is a
  heavily defensive WAR+CLR build.
- Warrior is the best tank (avoid WAR+MNK — heavy overlap). Berserker
  with PAL/SK roughly halves LoH / Harm Touch cooldowns and speeds
  their reave/smite extra attacks.
- Stance/invocation swapping is essential (Berserker + Inversion rate
  strongest).
- Motes: spend on SPELLS, not gear — the highest spell in a series or
  leveling accelerators (pet summons). Don't over-farm low +N gear;
  it's replaced fast from ~42. Fastest AA/mote income: level a fresh
  class 10-20 in D4 (white/yellow/red cons; named mobs drop the best
  motes; 8/25 made rares guaranteed, no placeholders, in Unrest and
  Solusek's Eye).
- Pets: strong under ~12; summon-spell TIER adds pet level (cap player
  -1; each level +6% HP, +1 base damage). Pet weapon rule: a pet swings
  weapon damage x2 when that beats its natural max hit — DELAY IS
  IRRELEVANT for pets. Pet gear persists across summons and zoning.
  8/25: pet PROC spell upgrades now carry to the pet (damage, resists),
  so tier the proc line; pets lost their own faction and aggro range —
  a parked pet no longer pulls by proximity.
- Any slots: Rogue -> best backstab dagger; everyone else -> shields.
- Raid ladder: Yael < Nagafen < Vox < Hate < Fear < Sky; Sky is the
  endgame hub (class unlocks + best gear).
