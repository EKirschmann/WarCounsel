# Druid (EQL)

Baseline distilled from the eqlwiki.com class page (2026-07-21); control
and upgrade mechanics from eqltools.com /learn/control and
/learn/spell-upgrades (2026-07-30, post-launch). Front-load
decision-relevant facts.

- Role: nature priest — heals, ports, damage shields, quad-kiting;
  solos and earns well ("money via transports, item farming, quad
  killing giants"). Meta ranks DRU alongside SHM behind Cleric.
- Ports: the widest network in Norrath (Ring/Circle/Zephyr lines, 15+
  destinations; group evacs). Ritual note: level a Druid to ~25 ONCE
  and its ports work from any loadout (see general guide).
- Heals: Minor -> Greater Healing -> Healing Water plus HoT line
  (Budding/Sprouting/Flowering/Blooming — final Bloom ~300);
  Regeneration L34.
- Damage shields: Thistles/Barbs/Brambles/Spikes on others,
  Thistlecoat->Bramblecoat self — passive damage on any tank.
- Stances/invocations: Channeler, Recovery, Divine (mana spent heals
  lowest-HP member), Empowering.
- Caveats: several key spells are OUTDOOR-only; animal charm to ~L43;
  fire/cold nukes + swarm DoTs; snare/root control.

## Control (eqltools /learn/control)
- Charm is ANIMALS ONLY for Druid and Shaman. Enchanter and Bard charm
  anything; Necromancer charms undead. Do not plan around charming a
  humanoid.
- Druid is both a ROOT class and one of the two EARLY snare classes
  (with Ranger); Bard/SHD/NEC/WIZ get snare later. Root classes: WIZ,
  DRU, ENC, CLR, SHM, NEC, RNG, PAL.
- **Raid bosses cannot be rooted or snared.** Any plan that opens with
  a snare fails on a boss — check before building around kiting.
- Upgrading a control spell raises the NPC LEVEL CEILING it can affect
  (mez, charm, pacify, fear, stun). Tier is not just duration: an
  un-upgraded root may simply not land on a higher mob.
- Slow and haste are FLAT values on an attack-speed stat, not the
  multiplicative percentages classic EQ used — 100 speed +60 haste =
  160; 65 slow takes it to 95.

## Spell tiers (eqltools /learn/spell-upgrades)
- Duration: +10%/tier for buffs and debuffs, +5%/tier for DoTs and
  HoTs. Rank 10 = 200% and 150% of base respectively.
- Resist modifier improves a flat -15 per tier; debuff and charm mana
  falls 4%/tier (2% for most other spells); nuke damage +6%/tier.
- Mote cost DOUBLES per tier (2^tier), so rank 8 is 128 and rank 10 is
  1024 — upgrade breadth before depth unless a specific spell is
  carrying the loadout.
