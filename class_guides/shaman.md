# Shaman (EQL)

Baseline distilled from the eqlwiki.com class page (2026-07-21). Replace/extend with community wisdom as it appears; front-load decision-relevant facts.

- Role: tribal support priest — "the most powerful slowing spells and
  resistance debuffs in the game" plus real heals; meta ranks SHM just
  behind Cleric and counts it as a SLOWER for D3+.
- Slows: Drowsy L5 -> Walking Sleep L13 -> Tagar`s Insects L27 (~50%
  attack-speed cut at the top). Haste: Quickness L26, Alacrity L42
  (+30-40%).
- Stances/invocations: Channeler (40% DR to mana/end), Recovery, Divine
  (mana spent heals lowest-HP member), Over Channel, Empowering.
- Toolkit: stat buff suite (Spirit of Wolf/Bear/Cat/Ox lines, +40 stats
  by L39-43 — no WIS/INT buffs), strong poison/disease DoTs, heals to
  456 at L40 + HoTs, Cannibalize HP->mana (L23), permanent pet at L32,
  animal charm to ~L33.
- Iksar shamans start KoS in many places (faction).

## Slow, the shaman's job (eqltools /learn/control, 2026-07-30)
- Slow/haste are FLAT attack-speed values, not multiplicative: a mob at
  100 with 65 slow sits at 95. Judge a slow by the number it subtracts,
  not a percentage.
- **Slows do not stack — the best one wins.** Landing a second slow on
  an already-slowed mob is wasted mana. The one exception is Rogue slow
  poison, which stacks additively at roughly 15%.
- Slow landing is a MAGIC RESIST CHECK. Debuff resists first: the
  Shaman Malo line, the Enchanter Tashan line, or the Overchannel
  invocation. On a resistant target that ordering is the whole fight.
- Upgrading a control spell raises the NPC level ceiling it can affect,
  so an un-upgraded slow may not land at all on a higher mob rather
  than landing weakly.
- Haste caps by era: ~50% below L30, ~75% at L50, 100% at Kunark tier.
  Item haste alone reaches the cap, so class haste matters while
  levelling and stops mattering once geared.
